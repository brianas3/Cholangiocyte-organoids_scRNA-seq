# 02_qc — QC filtering per Sampaziotis et al. 2021 Methods

**Question** Which cells/genes pass Sampaziotis et al. 2021's QC criteria when applied to the Atlas-hosted E-MTAB-8495 raw count matrix?

**Script** `scripts/02_qc.py`

## Inputs
- data/Result files/E-MTAB-8495-quantification-raw-files/*.mtx(+cols/rows)
- data/E-MTAB-8495_biosample_to_sample.csv

## What was done
- Gene filter: for each of the 3 origins (PRI/ORG/BTO), a gene is kept if it has >0 counts in >=3 cells in EVERY sample-batch of that origin; retained overall if it passes for >=1 origin (18,274/26,841 genes retained).
- Cell filter: per-batch (22 samples) scater::isOutlier-style 3-MAD detection on log10(total_counts) [lower], log10(n_genes_by_counts) [lower], and pct_counts_mt [higher] (median +/- 3*1.4826*MAD, the scater consistency constant). Mitochondrial genes = the 13 protein-coding MT- genes present in the matrix (Ensembl chrMT overlap query; 24 Mt_tRNA/Mt_rRNA genes were not present in the Atlas gene-filtered matrix).
- Additionally require counts>3 for at least one of EPCAM (ENSG00000119888), KRT7 (ENSG00000135480), KRT19 (ENSG00000171345).
- Paper's Methods do not specify log-transform or outlier direction explicitly; this follows the standard scran/OSCA convention.

## Outputs
- outputs/tables/02_qc_cells_per_sample.csv
- outputs/figures/02_qc_diagnostics.png (+ .pdf)
- outputs/objects/02_qc_filtered.h5ad (40,732 cells x 18,274 genes)

## Findings
- 40,732/72,803 raw barcodes pass QC.
- Organoid (ORG) and bile-treated organoid (BTO) counts land close to the paper (1.06-1.21x reported).
- PRI is off in both directions: IHD 357 vs paper's 587 (0.61x, consistent with 587 including a 5th 'patient' that is the external MacParland 2018 dataset not present in this accession), GB 2985 vs 3702 (0.81x), CBD 3974 vs 3006 (1.32x).
- Expected: the paper's own QC'd/integrated object was never deposited (only fastq were), so this re-derives QC from Atlas's independent re-quantification of the same fastq, not the authors' original CellRanger run.

## Still open
- Paper additionally removes small Louvain clusters enriched for mito/gene-count outliers AFTER clustering -- deferred to the clustering step.
- isOutlier direction/log-transform choice is an assumption (paper text is ambiguous).
